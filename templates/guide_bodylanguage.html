Body Language

Research consistently shows that non-verbal communication carries more weight than words alone. Your posture, eye contact, and gestures speak before you do.

The Handshake & Greeting

  • A firm but not crushing handshake signals confidence and professionalism.
  • Make eye contact and smile when introduced — it immediately creates warmth.
  • Wait to be invited to sit. This is a small detail that shows awareness and respect.

During the Interview

  • Sit up straight but not rigid. Lean forward slightly to show engagement.
  • Maintain natural eye contact — look at the interviewer when they speak and when you answer. Do not stare unblinkingly, but do not look at the floor either.
  • Keep hands visible and relaxed on the table. Avoid fidgeting, tapping, or playing with objects.
  • Nod occasionally to show you are listening and engaged.
  • Avoid crossing your arms — it can appear closed off or defensive.
  • Mirror the interviewer's energy level subtly. If they are calm and measured, match that.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Looking at your phone, even briefly, is an immediate red flag.
  • Slouching communicates disinterest or low confidence.
  • Excessive nodding looks nervous or insincere.
  • Avoiding eye contact entirely suggests dishonesty or anxiety.
